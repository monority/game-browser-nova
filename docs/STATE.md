## Current Approved Step

Removed from `BUILDING_DEFINITIONS`/`BuildingType`, the economy (`connectedMarkets`, `expectedMarketSales`, `sellMarketSurplus`, `marketSafetyStock`, `MONEY_PER_FOOD_SOLD`, the `market` field of `collectRevenue`), the stats/forecast/alerts views (`marketPerTick`, the food-balance drain, the `Market: 0/2 food sold` alert), the palette, the inspection detail, the renderer shape and the metadata table; its milestone `market1` left the age-2 set, so Age 2 is the four Step021 dimensions again. `tests/market.test.ts` (352 lines) and the `market safety floor` E2E spec went with it.

Measured consequences, all recorded rather than papered over: the age-2 and age-3 agent journeys were re-anchored to exact food/water balances (pop 16 → `HEALTHY` at t=780 with 787 money; pop 22 → `HEALTHY` at t=1140 with 3319, Age 3 open at t=1141) because the market used to absorb the +2 surplus — without it a 24-colonist line pins the granary and reads `critical`. The self-play governor lost its Step021 project mode (which existed only to afford the market's 2 planks) and gained a water triage (rest workshops when the cistern is below the population) because the food-drain triage that used to free workers never fires on a food-pinned colony; the run now reaches pop 24 with 10317 money.

Also measured in the same pass and REJECTED: making workshop commerce require staffing ("the most logical" of the three idle-industry options) — the opening falls to -2/tick and freezes at t=120, so it stays an open decision pending a D2 re-anchor. Validation: tsc, lint, 325 unit tests, E2E full suite, build.
